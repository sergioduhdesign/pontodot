Pontodot

Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026

Summary. Pontodot is a work management platform for agencies and communication teams. We use data only to make the platform work for the companies that hire it. We do not sell data. You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data at any time at pd@pontodot.ai.

1. Who is responsible

Pontodot is operated by NOVA HORDA SERVICOS & NEGOCIOS LTDA, a Brazilian company registered under CNPJ 43.383.376/0001-35, trade name PONTODOT (“Pontodot”, “we”). For any privacy matter, including contact with our data protection officer, write to pd@pontodot.ai.

2. What Pontodot does

Pontodot organizes tasks, approvals, schedules and content production for teams and their clients. When a client authorizes it, the platform also connects to the client’s Meta accounts (Facebook and Instagram) to publish content, reply to comments and direct messages, track the results of posts and ads, and receive the contacts (leads) generated by ad forms.

3. What data we process

  • Platform account: name, email, profile photo (if uploaded) and role in the team.
  • Platform usage: tasks, comments, files, approvals, conversations with the assistant and the work preferences the person chooses to provide.
  • Meta data, only when the account owner connects and authorizes it:
    • the list of Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts the person manages;
    • posts, comments, direct messages received by the connected account, and post metrics (reach, impressions, interactions);
    • ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads and their metrics (spend, reach, clicks, results and cost per result);
    • data people fill in on ad forms (leads), such as name, email and phone number.
  • Technical data: access and error logs, and the browser notification subscriptions of each device the person chooses to enable.

3-A. Agentic School waitlist

On the pontodot.ai website you can join the Agentic School waitlist. We ask for your name and email and, optionally, your WhatsApp number and the type of work you do. We use this data only to tell you when enrollment opens and to share school news. The legal basis is your consent (Brazilian LGPD, art. 7, I), recorded with date and time when you sign up. We do not sell this data or share it for third-party advertising. It is kept until the school opens and you decide whether to enroll, or until you ask to leave. To leave the list, correct or delete your data, write to pd@pontodot.ai (we reply within 15 days).

3-B. Measurements on pontodot.ai

The public page measures, anonymously, how many people visit, how far they scroll and which buttons they click, so we can improve the content. We do not use tracking cookies, we do not store your IP address and we do not link these measurements to your name. Each visit gets only a random code that disappears when you close the tab.

4. What we use it for

  • Making the platform work: showing tasks, deadlines, approvals and results to the right team.
  • Publishing and scheduling content, replying to comments and messages, and creating or adjusting campaigns, always at the request of whoever manages the account.
  • Showing post and ad metrics in reports for the team and for the client.
  • Delivering ad leads to the client that generated them, so the client can follow up.
  • Sending deadline reminders and the notifications the person enabled.
  • Generating the platform assistant’s answers from what the team itself asks.

We do not use Meta data to build profiles of people, to sell, or for any purpose other than those listed above.

5. Legal basis (LGPD)

We process data based on performance of the contract with the companies that use the platform, on the consent given when a Meta account is connected, on compliance with legal obligations and on the legitimate interest of keeping the platform secure and running, under Brazilian Law No. 13,709/2018 (LGPD).

For lead data and end-customer data, the advertiser company is the controller; Pontodot acts as the processor, handling data only on that company’s instructions.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent data. We share it only with providers needed to operate the platform, who process data on our behalf:

  • database and authentication infrastructure (Supabase);
  • website hosting (HostGator);
  • storage of work files (the user company’s own Google Drive);
  • artificial intelligence providers, which process the content sent only to generate the requested answer;
  • Meta itself, for the actions the person authorizes (publishing, reading metrics, receiving leads, replying to messages).

We may also share data when required by law or by order of a competent authority.

7. Where it is stored and how we protect it

Data is stored on cloud providers’ servers, which may be outside Brazil, with the safeguards the LGPD requires for international transfers. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access control by person and by role, and Meta access keys are kept on the server and never exposed in the browser.

8. How long we keep it

We keep data while the account or contract is active. Post and ad metrics are fetched from Meta when someone asks and are stored only when they are part of a report. Leads remain available to the client for the period agreed with them. At the end of the contract, or on request, we delete or anonymize the data, except what the law requires us to keep.

9. Your rights

At any time you may: confirm whether we process your data; access it; correct it; request anonymization, blocking or deletion; request portability; know who we share it with; and withdraw consent. Just write to pd@pontodot.ai. We reply within 15 days.

10. How to request deletion of your data

  • To disconnect your Meta account: on Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find Pontodot and click Remove. This cuts the platform’s access immediately.
  • To delete the data we already received: send an email to pd@pontodot.ai with the subject “Data deletion”, giving your name and the email or Meta account used. We confirm the request and complete the deletion within 15 days, notifying you by email.
  • If you filled in an ad form of a company that uses Pontodot, you can ask that company directly or us to delete it; we forward the request to the company.

11. Browser storage

The platform stores in your browser only usage preferences (such as the theme and workspace layout) and the login session. We do not use advertising cookies.

12. Children

Pontodot is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be announced inside the platform.
